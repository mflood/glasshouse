# Maintenance record extract: DSV Cormorant, August period

Compiled for the review board, 27 November.

The August maintenance period ran from 4 to 11 August aboard the Halvard Lyng,
at sea, on station over the eastern basin. The period was originally scheduled
for the shore facility at Vardstrand from 12 to 26 August. It was moved after
the Kestrel Rise survey was brought forward by six weeks, a decision recorded in
the programme change note of 14 June.

Work carried out in the period included: replacement of both penetrator
compression seals, port and starboard; inspection of the ballast release
mechanism; replacement of two thruster bearings; recharge and load test of the
reserve bus batteries; and routine hull survey.

The penetrator seal replacement is recorded as completed on 7 August by two
technicians, signed off the same day. The maintenance manual requires visual
inspection of the seat before the new seal is fitted. That inspection is
recorded as performed. The manual does not require the seat to be measured, and
no measurement was taken. Nor does it require photographic record, and none
exists.

The wet bay was not a controlled environment during the period. The record notes
that seal work was carried out with the bay open to the deck, which is standard
practice at sea, and that the sea state during the period ranged from two to
four.

Four previous penetrator seal replacements were carried out at sea in the
preceding two years: in October, the following March, that June, and the
following February. None was followed by a reported bus anomaly.

The shore facility at Vardstrand has a clean bench rated for this work. The
programme has used it for penetrator work on eleven occasions since the vehicle
entered service.
