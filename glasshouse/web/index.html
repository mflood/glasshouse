    What a verdict does and does not mean

    Grounded means withholding that chunk measurably changed the sentence — the model used it. It does not mean the chunk is true, or that the sentence reads it correctly.

    Model memory means the model produces the same claim with no documents at all. It may well be true. It did not come from your corpus, so your corpus is not evidence for it.

    Unsupported means neither the evidence nor the model's own knowledge accounts for it. Usually connective wording; occasionally a claim that could not be traced.

    Every effect is measured against a noise floor taken from a control run that saw exactly the same evidence in a different order, so ordinary rephrasing is not mistaken for grounding.
